Flash of Electric Reflection (5pcs Set): When the Resonator inflicts Electro Flare on enemies, they gain the following effects: Gain 10% Electro DMG Bonus for 15s. While this effect is active, casting Outro Skill grants the incoming Resonator 25% Electro DMG Bonus for 15s.

[Adjustments and Optimizations]
✦Features✦
●Backpack:
Optimized Echo Backpack Storage
- All unequipped, non-upgraded Echoes in your Echo Backpack that share the same Echo type, Sonata Effect, and Mainstat will automatically stack into a single backpack item.
- Each backpack item can hold up to 999 Echoes. The excess will be removed.
- Adjusted the placement of the pieces of information displayed on Echo icons.
- After Version 3.7 goes live, all your Echoes will automatically stack. Locked/Discarded Echoes that meet the stack requirements will stack according to the following rules.
①Locked Echoes and Non-locked Echoes will be marked "Locked" and stack together.
②Discarded Echoes and Unmarked Echoes will be marked "Discarded" and stack together.
③Locked Echoes and Discarded Echoes will be marked "Locked" and stack together.
 
●Echo Recommendation
Optimized Display of Echo Recommendation Screen
- On the Suggest screen for Echo Recommendation, with Resonators who have two Resonance Modes, Resonance Mode-specific Echo sets will be recommended. If a popular set is the same as the default recommendation, the corresponding Resonance Mode icon will be displayed.
- The above information will be synced to the Suggest menu for Echo Equipment.
 
●Team
Added Team Management
- You can drag to reorder your teams via the Team Management menu.
- You can also rename your teams. (The name of a team is displayed in the top left corner of the screen.)
- The number of team setups has been increased to 20.
 
Added Lineup 3D Display
- A 3D/2D button will appear in the top right corner of the Team screen, allowing you to switch between 3D and 2D display. (※2D is the default.)
- Select the 3D option to display your Resonators in 3D mode.
- Added the Preset Teams menu to the lineup page for Tower of Adversity, allowing you to select one of the Preset Teams for a challenge.
- 3D Display is not available on devices with the following configurations:
①iOS:
RAM≤ 4GB
②Android:
RAM≤ 6GB.
※Enabling 3D Display may contribute to the device load. Please choose the suitable option based on the performance of your device.
※The current 3D Display feature is not the final version. We will continue to refine this feature in further versions in terms of visuals and device load.
 
●Camera Mode:
Improved Functionality & Usability
- New UI, with the previously available features intact.
- Added more shooting modes and adjustable parameters.
 
New Shooting Modes
- Handheld mode, allowing you to adjust the character's line of sight.
- Selfie mode, allowing you to take photos of Rover in various poses and expressions. (※Only Rover is available in Selfie mode.)
- Added a drone view to the original shooting mode, allowing you to capture expansive panoramic views from great distances.
 
New Supportive Features
- Added reference lines, allowing you to better frame and adjust shots.
- Added lens templates, allowing you to select among the preset lens templates.
- Added Quick Focus, allowing you to focus the camera on your subject by clicking on it. After using Quick Focus, you can adjust the Focus Distance with your subject as a reference.
 
Added On-character Lighting Templates
- Select lighting templates to adjust the lighting effects on the current Resonator. You may adjust the Lighting Direction.
※We will continue to refine the on-character lighting parameters and bring you more options in this regard. Please stay tuned.
 
Added Environmental Settings
- You can adjust environmental settings such as Time, Weather, and Foliage Wind Direction in the Environment tab. (Changes to Time may take some time to take effect.)
- Added the Atmosphere effect option. You can adjust the effect density.
- With the Foliage Wind Direction option, you can adjust the wind direction, along with the wind strength. The wind direction and strength will affect how the foliage and the Atmosphere objects move.
- The Environmental Settings apply only to Camera mode.
 
Added Preset Frames
- You can add a preset frame to the photo you are taking.
- Added the selectable "Wuthering Waves" logo to the photo you are taking (the size of the logo is adjustable.)
 
Optimized Global Filter Settings
- In addition to the preset Filters in camera mode, there are also an adjustable Color Palette and other adjustable advanced parameters.
- The Global Filter settings in Camera mode do not affect the Global Filter settings for and the display of the Overworld.

②Dimensity Series: Dimensity 7200, Dimensity 7200-Ultra, Dimensity 8000, Dimensity 8100, Dimensity 8100-MAX, Dimensity 8200, Dimensity 8200-Ultimate, Dimensity 8200-Ultra, Dimensity 8250 Star Speed Edition, Dimensity 7300, Dimensity 7300-Ultra, Dimensity 8300-Ultra, Dimensity 8350, Dimensity 7500

- Extended support for self-developed frame generation to mobile devices equipped with Dimensity 9300 and Dimensity 9300+. If your processor meets the requirements, you can enable this option via Settings > Graphics > Frame Generation after enabling Vulkan.
- Running the game with the "Frame Generation" option enabled at 60FPS (maximum) consumes less power than running the game at 60FPS without the option enabled.
