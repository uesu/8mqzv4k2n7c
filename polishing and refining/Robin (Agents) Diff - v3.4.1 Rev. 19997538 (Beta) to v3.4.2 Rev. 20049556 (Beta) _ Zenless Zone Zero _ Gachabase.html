Special Attack: Off-Field

Press to activate:
Leap high into the air and unleash an Energy Blast around yourself, dealing massive Ether DMG.
During skill activation, Anti-Interrupt level increases, and DMG taken is reduced by 40%.
After activation, triggers a Quick Assist. After switching out, Robin enters the [Off-Field Guard] state for 80s. Repeated triggers refresh the duration. At the same time, grants all squad members a [Guardian Shield] equal to 2% of Robin's initial Max HP, lasting 15s.
While Robin is in the [Off-Field Guard] state, she cannot be manually switched in except via [Chain Attack], [Entry Skill], or [Counter Assist]. Robin exits the [Off-Field Guard] state upon being switched in.
During the [Off-Field Guard] state, all squad members gain a DEF boost. When Robin's initial Max HP exceeds 16,000, every 1,000 additional HP grants 80 DEF, up to a maximum of 800 DEF. This effect persists for 15s after Robin's [Off-Field Guard] state ends.

Special Attack: Aftershock

While Robin is in the [Off-Field Guard] state, she automatically activates this skill when the squad's active character hits an enemy with any attack:
Swings her staff to attack enemies in front, dealing Ether DMG;
Character is invulnerable while using this skill;
Upon activation, gains 20 [Entry Points] and grants all squad members a [Guardian Shield] equal to 4% of her initial Max HP for 15s;
[Special Attack: Aftershock] counts as an Aftershock and can be triggered at most once every 10s.

EX Special Attack: Enhanced Aftershock

When activating [Special Attack: Aftershock], if Energy is at least 40, it is upgraded to [EX Special Attack: Enhanced Aftershock]:
Continuously consumes a total of 40 Energy to fire a powerful laser forward, dealing massive Ether DMG.
Robin increases the heavy attack's DMG by an additional amount equal to 140% of her Max HP.
Character is invulnerable while using this skill.
Upon activation, gains 20 [Entry Points] and grants all squad members a [Guardian Shield] equal to 8% of her initial Max HP, lasting 15s.
[EX Special Attack: Enhanced Aftershock] is considered an Aftershock.

Entry Skill:

When at least 140 [Entry Points] are available, switching this character on-field or holding consumes 100 [Entry Points] to activate:
Unleashes an Energy blast at enemies in front, dealing massive Ether DMG;
This skill counts as a [Quick Assist] and exits the [Off-Field Guard] state upon activation;
Provides all squad members with a [Guardian Shield] equal to 4% of this character's initial Max HP, lasting 20s;
Triggers a Quick Assist upon activation. Switching characters within 1.5s causes Robin to automatically unleash [Special Attack: Off-Field].

Quick Assist:

When the active character is launched, press to activate:
Swing a staff to attack enemies in front, dealing Ether DMG.
Character is invulnerable while using this skill.

Defensive Assist:

When the on-field character is about to be attacked, press to activate:
Parries the enemy's attack, dealing massive Daze.
This skill excels at parrying enemy attacks and reduces the consumption of [Assist Points] when the character is under intense attacks.
Character is invulnerable while using this skill.

Assist Follow-Up:

Press after a [Defensive Assist] to activate:
Swings her staff to attack enemies in front, dealing Ether DMG.
Character is invulnerable while using this skill.
Triggers a Quick Assist upon activation. Switching characters within 1.5s causes Robin to automatically unleash [Special Attack: Off-Field].

Counter Assist: Grapple

When you or the on-field character is about to be hit by certain enemy Control Skills, press to activate:
Grapple with the enemy. On a successful grapple, neutralize the enemy's Control Skill, deal Ether DMG, and gain 1 [Buff Enhance Point].
Character is invulnerable while using this skill.

Assist Follow-Up: Grapple Synergy Strike

When other squad members activate a [Counter Assist] and trigger a [Shutdown], automatically activates:
Fires a powerful laser forward, dealing massive Ether DMG;
Character is invulnerable while using this skill;
[Assist Follow-Up: Grapple Synergy Strike] is considered an Aftershock.

Core Passive:

When Robin uses certain skills, she grants all squad members a [Guardian Shield] of varying strength based on her initial Max HP, lasting 15s. When a character gains [Guardian Shield] again, the shield amount stacks and its duration is extended, up to a maximum of 60s. The shield amount cannot exceed 12% of Robin's initial Max HP;
Each time a squad member gains [Guardian Shield], their DMG dealt increases by 25% for 20s. Repeated triggers refresh the duration;
Using [Special Attack: Off-Field] triggers a Quick Assist. After being switched out, Robin enters the [Off-Field Guard] state for 80s. While in [Off-Field Guard], Robin gains [Entry Points] when she unleashes Aftershocks, up to a maximum of 140 points;
When Robin has 120 or more [Entry Points], she can be actively switched in via her [Entry Skill]. Upon switching in, 120 [Entry Points] are automatically consumed to unleash her [Entry Skill], and Robin exits the [Off-Field Guard] state;
When using her [Entry Skill], Robin gains 1 [Buff Enhance Point], up to a maximum of 1;
Upon entering the [Off-Field Guard] state, if Robin has a [Buff Enhance Point], 1 [Buff Enhance Point] is consumed to further increase the DEF boost provided by [Off-Field Guard] and its cap by 25%;
If a Quick Assist is triggered after using an Entry Skill, Ultimate, Chain Attack, or Assist Follow-Up, and characters are switched within 1.5s, Robin automatically stays on the field to unleash [Special Attack: Off-Field], during which she is invulnerable;
Robin gains 1 [Buff Enhance Point] when performing a [Counter Assist] or [Chain Parry];
After a [Chain Parry], if Robin is not the active character, she automatically unleashes [Special Attack: Off-Field], during which she is invulnerable;
Each time any squad member triggers Maim, Robin recovers 15 Energy.

Upon entering the battlefield, gain 1 [Buff Enhance Point]. In Investigation Zone mode, this effect can trigger once every 180 seconds;
